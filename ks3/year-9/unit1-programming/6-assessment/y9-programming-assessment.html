Tab 3 — Mario Maze

move_left()Move one step left along the current platform
climb_up()Climb one level up a ladder (must be standing on a ladder cell)
climb_down()Climb one level down a ladder
jump()Jump over a barrel — moves Mario one row up then back down, useful when a barrel is blocking your path
