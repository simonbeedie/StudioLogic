Level 2

Crane Control — Over the Wall

🧱 Your mission

There is now a wall between the ball and the basket. If your cable or ball hits the wall while moving, the task fails.

You must raise the ball high enough to clear the wall before moving the crane across. This means the order of operations matters — you can't just move sideways with a dangling ball.

Task — clear the obstacle

Grab the ball at column 2 → raise it above the wall (height 3) → move to column 8 → lower into basket.

⚠ Wall at columns 4–5, height 3 Ball at column 2, row 5 Basket at column 8
  • Pick up the ball the same way as Level 1 — move over it, lower hook, grab.
  • Raise the hook enough that the ball clears the wall. The wall reaches to row 2, so your hook + ball must be at row 1 or above.
  • Move the crane to the right — the program checks if the ball would clip the wall as you pass over it.
  • Once past the wall, lower the ball and drop it into the basket.

The red wall blocks low-flying cargo. Raise the ball to row 1 or higher before passing columns 4–5.
