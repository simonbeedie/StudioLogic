Level 1

Crane Control — Simple Lift

🏗️ Your mission

You are programming a crane to pick up a ball and drop it into a basket. The crane moves along a track at the top. Its hook can be lowered and raised. You must write a sequence of commands to complete the task.

The crane always starts at the left side of the track with its hook fully raised.

Task

Move the crane over the ball → lower the hook to grab it → move to the basket → drop the ball in.

  • Use move_right(n) to slide the crane along the track. Each unit = one grid square.
  • Use lower_hook(n) to extend the cable downward.
  • Use grab() when the hook is at the same height as the ball to pick it up.
  • Use raise_hook(n) to lift the ball clear, then move_right(n) to the basket.
  • Use drop() above the basket to release the ball. If it's directly above, it falls in!

The crane track runs across the top. Ball and basket positions are marked with grid numbers.
