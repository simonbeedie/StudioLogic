Island Map — Simple Route

🗺️ Your mission

You are programming an explorer to navigate across the island. The map is divided into a grid — columns are labelled A–M and rows are labelled 1–10.

Your explorer starts at position F9. You need to write a sequence of instructions to guide them to J3, just below the lighthouse.

Task

Guide the explorer from F9 to J3 (one square below the lighthouse) using movement commands.

  • Click the command buttons to add instructions to your program.
  • Each command moves the explorer one cell in that direction. Use move_north(3) to move 3 cells at once.
  • When your program is ready, click Run program to watch your explorer move.
  • If they don't reach the lighthouse, read the feedback and try again!
