💡 Level 3 — How it works

        
When you press Load Ghost, Python runs your entire run(ghost) function and collects every ghost.move() call into a list. Then when you press Start Game, those moves play back one per tick — so loops produce the repeated steps you expect.

        
Ghost 1 starts at col 1, row 1 (top-left). Ghost 2 starts at col 13, row 13 (bottom-right). Pac-Man starts at col 7, row 7 (centre) and moves randomly. Plan a route that aims to intercept where Pac-Man is likely to be.

        
You can mix loops and single moves to build your route:

        
def run(ghost):
    for i in range(6):
        ghost.move(1, 0)  # right 6
    for i in range(6):
        ghost.move(0, 1)  # down 6
    ghost.move(1, 0)     # one more right

        
Extension: can you make your route loop — so the ghost keeps patrolling after its first sequence finishes? Add another set of loops to bring it back to the start and try again.
