🛸 Level 3 — Swarm Survival

    
Three aliens, three editors. The ship moves and fires bullets. Aliens must dodge bullets and avoid colliding with each other. But the real goal is to destroy the ship — move an alien up to row 0 in the ship's column to defeat it.

    
Use alien.ship_col to track the ship and alien.move(0, -1) to advance upward. Coordinate all three aliens — can one reach the ship while the others survive?

    

      
alien — level 3 additions

      
alien.othersList of the other two aliens — each has col and row

      
alien.others[0]["col"]Column of the first other alien

      
alien.bulletsSame as Level 2 — list of {"col", "row"} dicts

      
alien.move(x, y)Move one step — avoid landing on another alien's position

      
alien.stay()Stay put — only safe if no bullet is incoming in your column

Check alien.others to see where the other aliens are — don't move into their position:

    
for other in alien.others:

        # don't move right!

    
Priority order: 1) dodge bullets first, 2) avoid other aliens, 3) advance up towards the ship, 4) stay if safe. Use return after a move to stop executing further code that tick.

    
Extension: spread out — give each alien a preferred column range. Then send one alien upward (alien.move(0, -1)) towards alien.ship_col to destroy the ship!
